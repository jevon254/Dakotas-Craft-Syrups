Step-by-step recipes using Dakota's Craft Syrups. New recipes added regularly.

Iced Vanilla Bean Latte or Matcha

🍶 Vanilla Bean Syrup

Coffee

Ingredients

  • 1–2 tbsp vanilla bean syrup (adjust to your sweetness preference)
  • 1–2 shots of espresso or matcha
  • 6–10 oz milk of choice
  • Ice

Instructions

  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Add vanilla bean syrup to the glass.
  3. Pour espresso or matcha over the ice.
  4. Top with milk of choice and stir gently.
Add-on tip: Make a Vanilla Bean Cold Foam! Combine 1 tbsp vanilla bean syrup, 1 tbsp heavy cream, and 1 tbsp 2% milk. Froth or shake until foamy and pour over the top instead of stirring milk in.
🥤

Brown Sugar Shaken Espresso

🍶 Brown Sugar Vanilla Syrup

Coffee

Ingredients

  • 1–2 tbsp brown sugar vanilla syrup (adjust to your sweetness preference)
  • 3–4 shots of espresso
  • ⅓ cup milk of choice
  • Ice
  • Cinnamon for topping

Instructions

  1. Add espresso and brown sugar vanilla syrup to a cocktail shaker with ice.
  2. Shake vigorously for 15–20 seconds until frothy.
  3. Strain into a glass over fresh ice.
  4. Top with milk of choice and sprinkle with cinnamon.
✨

Brown Sugar Vanilla Fizz

🍶 Brown Sugar Vanilla Syrup

Mocktail

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp brown sugar vanilla syrup
  • 1 tbsp fresh squeezed lemon juice
  • 4 oz ginger ale
  • 2 oz club soda or sparkling water
  • Ice

Instructions

  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Add syrup and fresh lemon juice, stir to combine.
  3. Pour ginger ale over the ice.
  4. Top with club soda or sparkling water. Stir gently and serve.
Make it a cocktail: Add your alcohol of choice before the soda for a great cocktail version!
🐉

Dragon Fruit Fizz

🍶 Dragon Fruit Syrup

Mocktail

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp dragon fruit syrup
  • ½ tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • Lemon lime soda or sparkling water to top
  • Ice

Instructions

  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Add dragon fruit syrup and lemon juice.
  3. Top off with lemon lime soda or sparkling water.
  4. Stir gently and serve.

Spicy Churro Mule

🍶 Churro Syrup

Cocktail

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp churro syrup
  • Whiskey of choice
  • Ginger beer to top
  • Ice

Instructions

  1. Fill a glass or mule mug with ice.
  2. Add churro syrup and whiskey of choice.
  3. Top with ginger beer and stir gently.
  4. Optional: garnish with a cinnamon stick.

Churro Hot Chocolate

🍶 Churro Syrup

Hot Drink

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp churro syrup
  • Your favorite hot cocoa mix
  • Milk or water (per your cocoa mix instructions)

Instructions

  1. Prepare your favorite hot cocoa mix with milk or water as directed.
  2. Stir in 1 tbsp of churro syrup.
  3. Top with whipped cream or marshmallows if desired.
Tip: Try topping with our Toasted Marshmallow Syrup drizzle for a s'mores twist!
